# 🧩 Comprehensive Experience & Modular Bullet Bank

> **Purpose**: Paired with [`resumes/career_profile_bank.md`](career_profile_bank.md), this file is the **exhaustive factual repository** of your chronological employment history, core accomplishments, and specialized reserve bullets.
> - **Bank-First Architecture**: Store *every* verified employer, role, metric, and accomplishment bullet here first. Your general Master Resume (`resumes/[Your_Name]_Resume_Master.md`) is an un-tailored general summary synthesized from this bank, and role-tailored resumes (`applications/YYYY-MM-DD_.../`) assemble the highest-fit bullets from this bank to match specific job descriptions.
> - **Page-Fill Calibration (Rule 6)**: When a tailored resume overflows its target page count, demote less relevant bullets (they remain safely stored here). When a tailored resume is too sparse, pull additional verified bullets from this bank so the document neatly fills the target page count without awkward blank space.
> - **Integrity Rule (Rule 3)**: All employers, metrics, and bullets in this bank are part of your verified factual whitelist enforced by [`scripts/lint_resume_integrity.ps1`](../scripts/lint_resume_integrity.ps1). Keep `[Level 1/2/3]` depth tags here in the bank; strip them when rendering candidate-facing resumes.
