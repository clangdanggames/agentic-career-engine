# 🗂️ Resume Variants (Multi-Track Positioning)

> **Purpose**: Maintain dedicated master baseline variants for candidates pursuing 2 or 3 distinct professional tracks (e.g. *Platform Engineering* vs *Quality Infrastructure*, or *Director of Operations* vs *Senior Program Manager*).

---

## 🎯 Why Use Resume Variants?

When applying to different role families, a single resume trying to speak to both audiences often appears diluted. Maintaining distinct variants allows you to:
1. **Calibrate the Summary & Framing**: Tailor your executive headline and summary to speak directly to the primary discipline.
2. **Prioritize Relevant Achievements**: Lead with accomplishments that matter most to that specific functional hiring manager.
3. **Speed Up Tailoring**: When tailoring for a specific job, your AI assistant starts from the closest variant archetype rather than restructuring your core master from scratch.

---

## 📁 Naming & File Conventions

Store your variant baselines in this directory:
- `resumes/variants/[Your_Name]_Resume_[Track_1].md`
- `resumes/variants/[Your_Name]_Resume_[Track_2].md`

*(Note: The repository's `.gitignore` automatically excludes all `*_Resume_*.md` and `*.pdf` files in this directory to ensure your personal career documents stay 100% private).*

---

## 🤖 Directing Your AI Assistant:

> *"Generate a variant of my master resume tailored for [Track 2, e.g. Technical Program Management]. Emphasize cross-functional roadmap delivery, stakeholder consensus, and risk mitigation, and save it to `resumes/variants/[My_Name]_Resume_TPM.md`."*
