> - **Integrity Whitelist (Rule 3)**: Every skill, credential, and metric recorded in this bank is automatically whitelisted by [`scripts/lint_resume_integrity.ps1`](../scripts/lint_resume_integrity.ps1). Keep `[Level 1/2/3]` depth tags here in the bank for agent calibration; strip them when rendering candidate-facing resumes.
