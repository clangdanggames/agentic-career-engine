## ❓ Strategic Questions to Ask the Interviewer

### For Hiring Manager / Functional Lead:
1. *"What is the single most critical problem or bottleneck the person in this role must resolve during their first 90 days?"*
2. *"How does the team currently balance short-term operational firefighting against long-term strategic initiatives?"*
3. *"What does exceptional performance look like in this role at the 1-year mark?"*

### For Cross-Functional Peers / Collaborators:
1. *"Where do the biggest friction points or misalignments typically emerge when collaborating with this department?"*
2. *"What tool, workflow, or architectural change made the biggest positive impact on your daily workflow recently?"*

### For Department Head / Executive:
1. *"From your vantage point, what market shift or customer requirement will demand the biggest evolution from this team over the next 18 months?"*
2. *"How is the company positioning this division to stay differentiated against emerging competitors?"*
