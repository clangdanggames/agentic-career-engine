## 🛠️ How to Customize Your Boolean Strings

The general formula is:
```text
(site:boards.greenhouse.io OR site:jobs.lever.co OR site:jobs.ashbyhq.com OR site:myworkdayjobs.com) ("Target Title 1" OR "Target Title 2") ("Location" OR "Remote") -intern -junior -unpaid
```

- **Filter by Recency**: In Google, click **Tools** $\rightarrow$ **Any time** $\rightarrow$ select **Past week** or **Past month** to view newly opened requisitions before they get flooded with applications.
- **Filter by Compensation**: Add `"$150,000"` or `"$160k..$220k"` to surface requisitions that explicitly state your compensation band.
