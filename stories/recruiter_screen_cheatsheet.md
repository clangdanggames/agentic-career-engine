# 📞 Recruiter Screen & Initial Phone Interview Cheat Sheet

> **Purpose**: A quick-reference cheat sheet for initial 15–30 minute recruiter phone screens. Keep this open during calls to eliminate anxiety, deliver crisp answers, anchor your compensation targets, and reverse-interview the recruiter.

---

## 🎯 1. The 45-Second "Tell Me About Yourself" Pitch

*Tip: Ask your AI assistant to customize this pitch using your Master Resume and the target role's job description.*

> *"I am a [Target Title / Core Discipline] with [X] years of experience specializing in [Domain A], [Domain B], and [Domain C].*
> 
> *Most recently at [Current or Previous Organization], I led [Major Initiative], where I [mention key outcome, e.g. scaled platform capacity by 3x / reduced delivery cycle by 35% / managed a $15M operational budget]. Alongside that core track record, I've been focused on [mention current focus, specialized expertise, or recent initiative relevant to the JD].*
> 
> *What drew me to [Company Name] is your focus on [Company Initiative / Problem Space]. I'm looking for my next long-term home where I can bring my background in [Core Strength] to help your team [mention 1 high-priority goal from the JD]."*

---

## 💰 2. Navigating Compensation Questions (Protecting Your Targets)

Recruiters typically ask about salary in the first 10 minutes. Use these scripts to anchor high without getting screened out:

### When they ask: *"What are your salary expectations for this role?"*

> **Primary Script**:
> *"Based on the responsibilities of this position and current market data for a [Target Title] of my experience, I am targeting a base salary in the **$[Target Band, e.g. $150,000 to $175,000]** range. That said, I view compensation holistically, so I am flexible depending on total compensation, performance bonus, equity/benefits, and overall team fit."*

### If they push for a hard number or state a lower band:

> **Deflection / Anchoring Script**:
> *"My primary priority is finding the right organization and role where I can drive meaningful business impact. What is the approved budgetary range for this position on your end?"*

---

## ❓ 3. High-Leverage Reverse Questions (To Ask the Recruiter)

Always have 2–3 questions ready when they ask: *"What questions do you have for me?"*:

1. *"What is the main challenge or operational bottleneck driving the need to fill this role right now?"*
2. *"How does this team interface with executive leadership and cross-functional partners?"*
3. *"What does the interview loop look like from here, and what is your target timeline for making a hiring decision?"*
