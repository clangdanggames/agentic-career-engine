# High-Impact Outreach Templates

> **Core Principle**: Keep networking messages concise (under 120 words), authentic, low-friction, and respectful of the other person's bandwidth. Always offer an easy "out" so conversations feel warm and professional rather than transactional or demanding. Applicable across **any industry or profession**.

---

## 1. Warm Outreach to Former Colleague / Manager for Referral

**When to use**: You discover an open role at an organization where a trusted former coworker or manager is currently working.

```text
Hi [Name],

Hope you've been doing well! It's been great following what you've been up to at [Company].

I'm currently exploring my next career chapter and focusing on [domain / role type, e.g. clinical operations / senior product management / platform engineering / financial strategy].

I noticed [Company] has an opening for [Role Title] on the [Team/Department Name] team. Given my background in [mention 1 core strength relevant to role, e.g. scaling cross-functional programs / leading multi-million dollar budgets / driving customer retention], it looks like an exciting match.

If you're comfortable submitting an internal referral or introducing me to the hiring team, I'd really appreciate it. Absolutely no pressure either way—happy to share my resume or grab a quick virtual coffee to catch up regardless!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

---

## 2. Re-connecting with Past Colleague / Manager (General Catch-Up)

**When to use**: Reconnecting with an old colleague at a target organization before a specific role is even posted, or warming up your network.

```text
Hi [Name],

Hope all is well with you!

I'm starting to reconnect with former colleagues as I look toward my next career move (recently spent time recharging and focusing on [mention professional development, project, or key focus area]).

I'd love to hear how things are going for you at [Company] and what kind of initiatives your team is prioritizing these days. If you have 10–15 minutes for a quick catch-up sometime over the next couple of weeks, let me know!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

---

## 3. Direct Message to Hiring Manager / Recruiter for a Specific Role

**When to use**: You find an exceptional fit role on an ATS board or LinkedIn and want to message the hiring manager directly.

```text
Hi [Name],

I saw you're leading / hiring for the [Role Title] position at [Company].

With [X] years of experience driving [mention 1-2 key accomplishments, e.g. regional healthcare expansion and regulatory compliance / high-throughput distributed systems / national brand marketing campaigns], and deep expertise in [mention domain/tool stack, e.g. enterprise P&L management, regulatory audits, or modern cloud architecture], I believe I could immediately help your team [mention 1 specific challenge from the JD, e.g. scale operations while maintaining audit compliance].

I've submitted my application through the portal, but wanted to introduce myself directly. Would love to connect if you think my background could be a strong match.

Best,
[Your Name]
[LinkedIn URL / Portfolio Link]
```

---

## 4. Replying to an Inbound Recruiter

**When to use**: A recruiter messages you on LinkedIn or email about a potential role.

```text
Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out! The [Role Title] role at [Company] looks intriguing, particularly the focus on [mention specific aspect, e.g. organizational transformation / product innovation / operational scale].

I'm currently exploring opportunities focused on [Senior/Staff/Lead/Director] level roles in [specialization], with a target compensation of [e.g. $140k+ base / Remote or Local Metro].

If this opportunity aligns with those parameters, I'd be glad to set up a brief introductory call to discuss further. Here is my current resume: [Link / Attached].

Best,
[Your Name]
```
