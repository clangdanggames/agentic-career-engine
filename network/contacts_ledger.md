## 💡 Quick Tips for Network Activation
1. **Export Connections**: Download your LinkedIn archive (`Settings -> Data Privacy -> Get a copy of your data -> Connections`).
2. **Analyze Overlap**: Run `powershell -File scripts/parse_connections.ps1` to automatically surface connections at top tech and target companies.
3. **Log & Match**: Populate this ledger with your highest-trust connections for automatic cross-referencing during ATS job scans.
