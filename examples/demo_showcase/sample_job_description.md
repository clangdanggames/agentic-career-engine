## 🏢 About NovaTech Systems
NovaTech builds enterprise developer productivity tools and scalable real-time event platforms serving over 40 million developers and end-users worldwide. We are an engineering-first culture focused on high autonomy, rigorous system design, and building reliable distributed infrastructure.

---

## 🎯 About the Role
We are seeking a **Senior Platform Engineer** to join our Core Developer Infrastructure team. In this role, you will design, scale, and optimize the distributed backend systems, event pipelines, and internal developer platforms that empower our global engineering organization to ship resilient code with high velocity.

---

## 🛠️ Key Responsibilities
- Architect, build, and maintain highly scalable backend microservices and streaming event pipelines using **Go**, **Kafka**, and **Kubernetes**.
- Own core developer platform tooling, continuous deployment infrastructure, and ephemeral preview environments using **Terraform** and **GitHub Actions**.
- Partner with product engineering teams to diagnose and optimize system bottlenecks, reducing p99 API latencies and improving distributed tracing coverage using **OpenTelemetry** and **Prometheus**.
- Enforce automated quality gates, load testing suites, and disaster recovery failover protocols across multi-region **AWS** deployments.
- Mentor intermediate and junior engineers through technical design reviews, code reviews, and post-incident reviews.

---

## 📋 Qualifications
- **Experience**: 5+ years of production software engineering experience building and operating distributed backend services at scale.
- **Languages**: Deep proficiency in **Go**, with secondary experience in **Python** or **TypeScript**.
- **Infrastructure**: Hands-on mastery of container orchestration (**Docker**, **Kubernetes** / EKS) and Infrastructure-as-Code (**Terraform**).
- **Messaging & Storage**: Experience with distributed event logs (**Kafka**) and relational/NoSQL databases (**PostgreSQL**, **Redis**, **DynamoDB**).
- **Observability**: Proven experience implementing metrics, distributed tracing, and structured logging (**Prometheus**, **Grafana**, **OpenTelemetry**).
- **Mindset**: Strong architectural judgment, ownership mindset, and excellent written and asynchronous communication skills.

---

## 🌟 Nice to Have
- Previous experience migrating monolithic services to event-driven microservice architectures.
- Experience with eBPF profiling or low-latency networking tuning.
